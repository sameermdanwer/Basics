The Common Entrance Exam (CEE) for Agniveer recruitment was conducted between June 30 and July 10, 2025, in online mode across the country. Since then, the internet has been flooded with videos carrying titles like “Result Kab Aayega?” or “Agniveer Result Out Now!” — most of which offer no official source or credible update. This has led to confusion and unnecessary anxiety among candidates and parents. The result is expected to be released in the last week of July, but even this is a tentative window. The Indian Army has not confirmed any final release date, and any updates shared outside the official website must be treated with skepticism.
